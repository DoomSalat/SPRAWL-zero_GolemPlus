# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public version.

- Sprint keeps the object held by the gravity gloves.
- Syringes can be pulled and used with the gravity gloves button.
- Kill marker with a sound.
- No movement slowdown while aiming.
- Alt scan: highlights enemies (not allies), relic fragments and codex tablets within 25 m, only when the ability is ready.
- Punch with a weapon in hand (mouse 4 by default).
- In-game settings panel on F1, choices saved to `settings.cfg`.
